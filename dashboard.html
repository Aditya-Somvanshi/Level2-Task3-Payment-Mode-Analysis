Reading the numbers. Revenue means the Sales column. Average order value is revenue divided by orders. Profit margin is Profit divided by Sales. May 2025 only runs to the 15th, so its bar and line points are partial.

Are the differences real? On the full dataset, an ANOVA found no significant difference in order value between payment modes (p = 0.39), and a chi-square test found that payment mix does not depend on region (p = 0.50). The modes are used almost equally, and regional differences are within normal random variation.
